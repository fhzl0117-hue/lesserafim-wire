SEOUL — AUGUST 2026

Five voices,
one long celebration.

LE SSERAFIM kicked off their "Time To Celebrate" era in April with the singles "Celebration" and "Boompala," then folded both into PUREFLOW Pt. 1 — an 11-track, 27-minute second studio album released May 22 that opened at No. 1 on Korea's Circle Chart. The rollout is being carried by the PUREFLOW World Tour, a 32-show, 23-city run that opened in Incheon in July and has since worked through Japan, with a Fukuoka stop, a BlizzCon 2026 closing set, and the group's first-ever solo European concerts still ahead. In the middle of all that, a second single album — Made My Night — was just announced, with title track "You Just Made My Night" landing September 11. This page is a working build of how LE SSERAFIM Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking FEARNOT — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

3.8 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"A Macarena sample and a hardstyle drop share the same eleven tracks — messy on paper, and mostly a lot of fun in practice."

PUREFLOW Pt. 1 (2026): LE SSERAFIM's second studio album, and their most deliberately cheerful concept to date. Where the group built its name on bold, empowering electropop and R&B, this "Time To Celebrate" era swaps a chunk of that edge for festival-ready maximalism — "Boompala" leans into Latin house and a sample most listeners will recognize instantly, while "Celebration" goes the opposite direction into melodic techno and hardstyle.

Coverage has focused more on chart performance than critical scoring so far — a No. 1 debut on Circle Chart and Top 3 finishes across several European markets — with members themselves describing heavier creative involvement in the record's production than on past releases.
